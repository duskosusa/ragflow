## Manual cleanup ledger

Use root [MANUAL-CLEANUP.md](MANUAL-CLEANUP.md) as the sole pending queue for verified obsolete files or folders that automatic cleanup could not remove. This canonical root file is an exception to general documentation-placement rules.

- Record the date, exact path, evidence that it is obsolete, the blocking reason, and the safe removal condition or procedure. Keep secrets and private document contents out of the ledger.
- Recheck listed paths before acting; remove an entry as soon as its target is absent or its cleanup is verified complete. Keep completed receipts as dated history and required resources out of the pending queue.
- Preserve unrelated dirty work, private data, backups, active consumers, dependencies and recovery evidence. If approval policy rejects deletion, record it here and stop that deletion; do not retry through another tool or method. A ledger entry alone does not authorize removal.
