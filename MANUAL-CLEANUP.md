## Maintaining this queue

- Add only a confirmed outstanding target. Record the date, exact path, why it is obsolete, the reason automatic deletion stopped, and the safe removal condition or procedure. Do not include secrets or private document contents.
- Before removal, recheck the exact path, ownership, Git recovery, active consumers and reparse points. Preserve unrelated work, private data, backups, required dependencies and recovery evidence. A listed path alone is not deletion authority.
- If approval policy rejects removal, keep the entry and stop that deletion; do not retry through another tool or method.
- Remove an entry immediately after verifying that the target is absent or cleanup is complete. Keep any useful completed receipt in the established history location, rather than retaining a resolved row here.
